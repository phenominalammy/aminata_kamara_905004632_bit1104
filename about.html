About Sierra Leone Football

Learn about our history, mission, and commitment to developing football in Sierra Leone.

Our History

The Sierra Leone national football team, known as the Leone Stars, represents Sierra Leone in international football and is controlled by the Sierra Leone Football Association (SLFA). The team has a rich history dating back to its first international match in 1949.

Despite facing challenges, the Leone Stars have shown remarkable resilience and talent on the international stage, with notable performances in Africa Cup of Nations tournaments and World Cup qualifiers.

Our most successful period came in the 1990s when we qualified for the Africa Cup of Nations twice, in 1994 and 1996, reaching the quarter-finals in 1996.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop, promote, and organize football in Sierra Leone at all levels, with the aim of fostering unity, promoting healthy lifestyles, and achieving sporting excellence.

We are committed to:

  • Developing youth talent through grassroots programs
  • Providing professional training facilities and coaching
  • Promoting gender equality in football
  • Building international partnerships and collaborations
  • Enhancing the fan experience during matches

Through these efforts, we aim to make Sierra Leone a competitive force in African and world football while using the sport as a tool for social development.
