Frequently Asked Questions

How can I purchase tickets for matches?

Tickets can be purchased online through our website (coming soon) or at the National Stadium ticket office on match days. For international matches, tickets will also be available at SLFA headquarters.

How can I try out for the national team?

National team selections are based on scouting and performance in domestic leagues. Players can also attend open trials announced by the SLFA. Keep an eye on our website and social media for trial announcements.

How can I become a sponsor or partner?

We welcome corporate partnerships and sponsorship inquiries. Please contact our commercial department at partnerships@sierraleonefootball.sl with details about your proposed collaboration.
